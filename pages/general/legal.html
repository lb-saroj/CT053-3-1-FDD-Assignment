Privacy Policy

LBEF Global Connect ("we", "our", "the club") respects the privacy of our members and website visitors. This policy explains what personal information we collect and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

  • Registration details: name, student ID, program, year, email, phone number.
  • Contact form submissions: name, email, and your message.
  • Newsletter email: only the email address you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and manage your club membership.
  • To send event invitations, newsletters, and important updates.
  • To respond to your enquiries submitted via the contact form.
  • To improve our events and services based on feedback.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Information may be shared with LBEF College administration and APU Malaysia only when directly relevant to your academic transfer process, and only with your consent.

4. Data Security

Your data is stored securely and accessible only to authorised committee members. We take reasonable technical measures to prevent unauthorised access.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. To do so, contact us at global@lbef.edu.np.
