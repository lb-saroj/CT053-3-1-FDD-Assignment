Our Mission

To bridge the gap between local education and global opportunities by connecting LBEF students with international academic partners, cultural exchanges, and career networks.

Our Vision

A future where every LBEF student has access to world-class education and the global mindset needed to thrive in an interconnected world.

Who We Are

LBEF Global Connect is the official student club of Lord Buddha Education Foundation (LBEF), Kathmandu, Nepal. We were established to support students who wish to take advantage of the partnership between LBEF and Asia Pacific University (APU), Malaysia.

Through our club, students gain access to information about credit transfer, housing in Malaysia, scholarship opportunities, and networking events with alumni who have successfully made the transition.

LBEF–APU Partnership: Students completing their first year at LBEF can transfer to APU Malaysia and receive full credit recognition, completing their degree in a globally ranked university.

What We Do

Workshops & Seminars

Regular sessions on study skills, IELTS preparation, visa guidance, and life abroad.

Networking Events

Connect with seniors, APU alumni, and international students at our organised events.

Application Support

Guidance on admission documents, credit transfer applications, and scholarship essays.

Our Values

  • Inclusivity — We welcome students of all backgrounds and programs.
  • Excellence — We push each other to achieve the highest academic standards.
  • Community — We build lifelong friendships and professional networks.
  • Integrity — We provide honest, accurate information to every student.
