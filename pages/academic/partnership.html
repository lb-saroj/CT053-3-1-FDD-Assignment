About Asia Pacific University (APU)

Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU) is one of Malaysia's premier private universities, located in Bukit Jalil, Kuala Lumpur. APU is consistently ranked among the top universities in Malaysia and is recognised by QS World University Rankings for its technology and innovation programs.

Why APU?

  • Top-ranked tech university in Malaysia
  • Strong industry connections and internships
  • International student community (120+ nationalities)
  • Modern campus with state-of-the-art facilities
  • Globally recognised British degree (UK validation)
  • Cost of living lower than UK/Australia/USA

How the Partnership Works

LBEF and APU have a formal Credit Transfer Agreement. Students who complete Year 1 at LBEF College are eligible to transfer directly into Year 2 of an equivalent undergraduate program at APU, with their credits fully recognised.

This means you save one full year of tuition fees at APU by completing your first year in Nepal before going to Malaysia.
