Credit Transfer Guide

Step-by-step process for transferring from LBEF to APU Malaysia

Home › Academics › Credit Transfer

Tip: Start the transfer process at least 6 months before your intended intake date. APU has two main intakes per year: February and October.

Eligibility Requirements

Requirement Minimum Standard
Minimum CGPA 2.5 / 4.0 (some programs require 2.75+)
English Proficiency IELTS 5.5 overall (or equivalent APU internal test)
Year 1 Completion Full Year 1 completed at LBEF in the relevant program
No Pending Papers Must have no outstanding failed modules from Year 1

Step-by-Step Transfer Process

1

Check Your Eligibility

Confirm your CGPA meets the minimum requirement for your target program. Visit the FAQ page or contact our Academic Liaison.

2

Prepare Your Documents

Collect all required documents:

  • Official academic transcripts from LBEF (stamped & signed)
  • Passport (valid for at least 18 months)
  • IELTS certificate (or sit APU's internal English test)
  • Completed APU application form
  • Personal statement (500–800 words)
  • Two referee letters (including one from LBEF faculty)
3

Submit Your Application to APU

Apply online via APU's admissions portal or through the LBEF academic office. LBEF students get priority review under the partnership agreement.

4

Receive Offer Letter & Pay Deposit

Once accepted, you will receive an official offer letter. Pay the acceptance deposit to secure your place (usually within 30 days of the offer).

5

Apply for Student Visa

APU will guide you through the Malaysian student visa (EMGS) process. Attend our Visa Workshop for help with documentation.

6

Travel & Enrol at APU!

Fly to Kuala Lumpur, complete orientation, and begin your studies at APU. Our alumni network will be there to help you settle in.

Important: Do not submit your visa application without the official APU offer letter and EMGS approval number.
