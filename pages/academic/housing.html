Accommodation Options

APU On-Campus Hostel

APU provides limited on-campus hostel rooms. Fully furnished with Wi-Fi, meals, and 24-hour security. Ideal for first-semester students.

~RM 500–700 / month

Shared Apartment (Off-campus)

Shared apartments in Bukit Jalil or Sri Petaling are popular with LBEF students. Usually 3–4 students share a unit near APU.

~RM 400–550 / month (per person)

Student Condominiums

Several student-friendly condominiums (e.g. CitiZen, The Z Residence) are a 5–15 minute walk from APU. Modern units with gym and pool access.

~RM 600–900 / month

Tips for Finding Housing

  • Join LBEF alumni WhatsApp groups — seniors often share housing leads and may have a spare room.
  • Check PropSocial, iProperty, or Mudah.my for available rental listings near Bukit Jalil.
  • Book early — arrange accommodation before you fly. Do not arrive without a confirmed place to stay.
  • Read the tenancy agreement carefully before signing. Look out for advance deposits (usually 2 months).
  • APU's International Office can also provide a list of verified housing partners upon request.

Best neighbourhoods: Bukit Jalil (walking distance), Sri Petaling (10 min MRT), and Puchong (15 min by bus) are the most popular with LBEF students.
