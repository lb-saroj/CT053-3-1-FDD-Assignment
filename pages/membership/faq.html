Frequently Asked Questions

Membership

Any currently enrolled student at LBEF College, Kathmandu can join. We welcome students from all programs — BSc Computing, BSc IT, BBA, BCA, and others. Membership is free of charge.
No, membership is completely free for all LBEF students. You simply need to complete the online registration form and you will be added to our member list.
Members receive: priority access to events and workshops, one-on-one guidance from senior students on the APU transfer process, club membership certificates, volunteer hour records, and recommendation letters from our Faculty Advisor.

APU Credit Transfer

LBEF College has a formal credit transfer agreement with Asia Pacific University (APU) in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Students who complete Year 1 at LBEF can transfer to APU and have their credits fully recognised, allowing them to continue in Year 2 of an equivalent degree program at APU.
Generally, a minimum CGPA of 2.5 (or 50% overall) is required for credit transfer eligibility. Some programs at APU require a higher GPA. Contact our Academic Liaison or LBEF's academic office for program-specific requirements.
If your medium of instruction at LBEF was English, you may be exempt from an IELTS requirement. However, APU may still request an internal English assessment. We recommend aiming for IELTS 5.5 or above to be safe. Our club runs IELTS preparation workshops regularly.
The process typically takes 3–6 months from application to receiving your offer letter. You should start the process at least 6 months before your intended intake date. Visit our Transfer Guide page for a detailed step-by-step timeline.

Events & Activities

All upcoming events are listed on our Events Calendar. For most events, you can register by contacting us via the contact form or by messaging us on our social media pages. Some events require prior registration due to limited seating.
Most of our events are open to all LBEF students, even non-members. However, members get priority registration and sometimes discounted or free access to premium workshops. We encourage everyone to join — it's free!
